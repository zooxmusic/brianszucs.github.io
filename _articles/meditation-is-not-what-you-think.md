You’ve already experienced meditative states. You just didn’t call them that. That moment you zoned out watching rain. That deep flow state while coding. That quiet calm on a walk.

**That’s meditation**. The difference? Meditation trains you to return there on purpose.

You don’t need incense. You don’t need lotus pose. You don’t need to chant (though chanting does have its place).

There’s really only one rule:

🪑 Keep your spine straight. Because if you slump, you’ll fall asleep—and this is about waking up.

🧠 Meet Your Mind: The Trainer and the Puppy
Inside you, there are two parts:

* 👨🏫 The Trainer (the observer)

* 🐕 The Puppy (your attention)

### The Trainer’s job is simple: Gently guide the Puppy to focus on the breath.

Not just “in” and “out”—but the whole experience of breathing. Warmth. Movement. Pressure. Sound. All of it.
	* But the Puppy doesn’t stay.
	* It chases squirrels like:
	* “Did I pay that bill?” “Oh this would make a great post…”
Your job?

🫶 “No no, Puppy. Back to the breath.”

Repeat. For the rest of your life.


💡 Real Talk: (as opposed to fake talk 😁) If You Train a Puppy Once a Week…
…it’s not gonna listen.

Train it 5 minutes a day? It starts paying attention. Train it an hour a day? You’ll be amazed. Eventually, the Puppy just looks at you and sits.

And then—it’s like it reads your mind. Because in a way, it is.

🧘♂️ Meditation isn’t about being perfect. It’s about showing up.

Every day. Back to the breath. Back to the moment. Back to you.
